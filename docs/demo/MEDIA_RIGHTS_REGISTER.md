## Privacy check

Before recording the demo, review the approved media for:

- Home addresses, licence plates, passports, boarding passes, or travel booking details.
- Minors or bystanders without permission.
- Exact live travel dates or other information that should not be published.
- Faces or text that should be blurred, removed, or held back.
